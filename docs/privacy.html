Privacy policy

fitness-coach is a personal app. Each copy runs on its owner's own computer, and the developer receives no data from it.

What it accesses in Google

One permission: calendar.app.created. With it, the app creates one "Training" calendar and adds, updates and removes its own events there. It cannot see or change any other calendar, and it has no access to email, contacts, files or anything else in the Google account.

Where data goes

Workout and body-measurement data, and the Google sign-in token, are stored only on the owner's computer. What the app writes to the Training calendar (session times, workout descriptions and a weekly summary) goes only to the owner's own Google account. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is used only to provide the feature above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
